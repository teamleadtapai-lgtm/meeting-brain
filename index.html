Example
In your ear

They lose Saturday orders because everything is on paper, and they cannot see stock across three shops. Propose one shared order tablet per shop with a live stock view, running before December. Ask how many orders they take on a busy Saturday.

What they need

  • Stop losing phone orders on busy Saturdays
  • See stock across all three bakeries
  • Simple enough for staff who avoid computers
  • Running before December

Solution to propose

A shared order app on one tablet per shop, with large buttons and a live stock count every shop can see. Start with orders only and add stock in week two, so staff learn one thing at a time.

Suggestions

  • Offer a two-week pilot in the busiest shop first
  • Include 30 minutes of staff training per shop in the price
  • Point out that a December start means deciding this month

Ask them next

  • How many orders do you take on a busy Saturday?
  • Who would look after it in each shop?
  • Do you have a budget in mind?

Tips for iPhone
  • Open this page in Safari, and keep it open with the screen on. Listening stops when the phone locks.
  • If the iPhone asks for the microphone again and again: tap the page menu in the address bar, then Website Settings, then set Microphone to Allow.
  • Dictation must be on: Settings, General, Keyboard, Enable Dictation.
  • With wireless earphones the iPhone listens through their microphone. Try it once before a real meeting.
  • For a Home Screen icon: tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
